# Experiment figures

## W1/W2 figure specifications

Figure numbers match the paper: W1 curves and field panels are Figure 2A/2B, W2 curves and field panels Figure 3A/3B, and W3 parameter identification Figure 4. Prespecified trajectories and layouts follow the declared project protocol.

Figures are generated from the corresponding complete numerical reports and actual prediction arrays. W1 has seven methods and W2 six, with ANIE participating only in W1; each method has one fixed training instance. Main methods use N300 and GIFT-Lite uses the first 50 entries of the ordered N300 list. Shared observations span t5–8 at dt=0.04 with 76 points; inference receives each trajectory's own single true t5 field. See the [training protocol](W1_W2_PROTOCOL.md).

W1 selects the nearest valid 0.04-second-grid nodes to the original seven report positions, choosing the earlier node in ties: t5/5.48/6/6.48/7/7.48/8. Markers, PCHIP inputs and CSVs all use actual times; intermediate curve positions provide visual trends only. W2 uses ten future report positions: t5.08/5.20/5.28/5.40/5.48/5.60/5.68/5.80/5.88/6. Main-table future75/future25 metrics still use every unknown node and do not change with figure sampling.

Confirmation and exploration sets each contain 180 trajectories and are plotted separately. Both populations are independent of training and validation, but both sets' scores have been viewed, so this is a non-blind post hoc evaluation. W1 fixes confirmation ID100005 and exploration ID1045, the 6th trajectory in each set; W2 fixes confirmation ID100110 and exploration ID1150, both at index 110. IDs and times are not selected by error. GIFT and GIFT-Lite field panels fix seed20260820; ANIE's original t5 reconstruction and nonzero error are shown in full.

U-NO is supervised for 20 steps to t5.80 and U-Net for 4 steps to t5.16; other methods have their declared objectives and native losses. GIFT-Lite retains GIFT's phase epochs with less data and fewer total updates. GIFT supervision lags are 0.08/0.48 seconds, with internal RK4 dt=0.02 and outputs saved every 0.04 seconds. Conclusions are limited to these fixed recipes and cannot be attributed solely to architecture or treated as an equal-budget comparison.

## Field-panel color scales and error definitions

W1/W2 use signed error prediction−truth, with RdBu_r for fields and PuOr for errors, both symmetric around zero. Selected W1 panels across both evaluation sets share field limits ±19 and error limits ±22, covering the maximum absolute residual 21.354568. W2 follows the shared rule max(default,ceil(actual maximum absolute value)), with field default 13 and error default 7: actual confirmation limits are ±16/±18 and exploration limits ±14/±20.

All methods in a figure share one scale per quantity. Native vector pcolormesh covers the full spatial domain, retaining W1's upward y-axis and W2's original upper-origin orientation. Source arrays are not flipped, finite values are not clipped, scales are not adjusted per method, and fields are not cropped or smoothed. Reference error cells retain the original light-gray zero-error placeholders. Nonfinite values or failures retain their method positions and labels; finite subsets cannot replace full-population results.

## W3 and S3 figure status

Figure 4 uses Tukey boxplots: boxes show Q1–Q3, center lines show medians, and whiskers end at the outermost actual observations within 1.5×IQR fences, consistent with the default 1.5 IQR definition in the [official Matplotlib documentation](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.pyplot.boxplot.html). The [NIST handbook](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/boxplot.htm) describes quartile boxes and median lines. All 50 actual observations are overlaid, including those beyond the whiskers; such points are not necessarily invalid and are not removed.

Figures 4a, 4b and 4c show absolute percentage errors for ν, β and γ, respectively, with APE_p=100×|p̂−p|/|p| calculated individually from the same 750 truth/estimate rows. Each figure orders GIFT, PDE-FIND, PDE-FIND-KC, PINN-SR and PINN-SR-KC consistently, retaining all 50 actual readouts per method for 250 points. Each point's horizontal coordinate is that readout's APE; points are sorted by APE and placed deterministically on seven equally spaced horizontal lanes, preferring an available central lane without collisions, although dense values may overlap. Dots have nominal diameter 2.1 pt and adjacent lanes are separated by 2.7 pt for a compact point layer. Each real point consists of 12 concentric, stroke-free, same-color transparent discs, with a radial Gaussian alpha profile for soft edges (sigma 0.42 relative to radius; nominal central alpha 0.95). This softens individual real markers without KDE, data smoothing or artificial observations. Lanes only organize points and do not encode a second variable; horizontal coordinates are not jittered, binned or rounded. Solid Tukey boxes represent the 25th–75th percentiles (Q1–Q3) of individual APEs; the extended vertical line through each box shows the median of 50 APEs, also reported by the Median value at the right of each row. With IQR=Q3−Q1, thin whiskers and short caps extend to the smallest and largest actual observations within inclusive fences [Q1−1.5×IQR, Q3+1.5×IQR], rather than ending at fence values. Actual observations beyond the whiskers retain their original coordinates, without duplicate flier symbols; means are not separately marked and the plots do not show confidence intervals. All statistics are computed after taking individual absolute values and describe empirical distributions across parameter combinations, not error bars across training seeds. Five methods share each figure's linear horizontal axis starting at zero: [0,300]% for ν and β, [0,110]% for γ, retaining all finite values. Each figure contains one full-range panel. GIFT is saturated dark blue, PDE-FIND dark orange, PDE-FIND-KC dark green, PINN-SR dark purple and PINN-SR-KC dark teal, with dots and whiskers sharing method colors. Method names are bold black and axes solid black; dots, solid boxes and whiskers use their saturated dark method colors. Median lines exactly match box colors, are 1.6 pt wide and extend through and beyond the boxes to about 2.1 times their height. Horizontal ticks point upward into the plot while numerical labels remain below the axis; the axis extends beyond the final tick by 3% of its current span without changing data coordinates or tick positions. Whiskers are thin and caps short. Plot text is limited to method names, coordinate ticks, parameter-specific horizontal-axis labels and each method's numerical Median value; statistical definitions are explained in the text. All three figures and the paper's main table share the same APE definition, allowing parameter-specific comparison of identification error magnitude and spread without expressing correlations between parameters. Individual errors and empirical statistics appear in the [coordinate CSV](../results/formal/W3_single_trajectory_20261010/figures/parameter_distributions/source_points.csv) and [statistics CSV](../results/formal/W3_single_trajectory_20261010/figures/parameter_distributions/source_statistics.csv); complete plotting rules and file identities appear in [plot metadata](../results/formal/W3_single_trajectory_20261010/figures/parameter_distributions/figure_metadata.json) and the [figure manifest](../results/formal/W3_single_trajectory_20261010/figures/parameter_distributions/figure_manifest.json).

## Plotting entry points

W3 uses the [parameter-specific absolute percentage error plotting entry point](../experiments/formal/w3_equation_identification/plot_parameter_distributions.py) to render Figure 4a/b/c directly from summary/parameter_results.csv. It reads only the saved 750 parameter-result rows and outputs three SVGs and PNGs, actual APE coordinates, 15 method/parameter empirical-statistics groups and complete plotting metadata, without training or recomputing parameter readouts. It refuses to overwrite existing output directories and preview files; --help lists input and output arguments.

W1/W2 figures are saved directly in their scientific result packages; `scripts.verify_published_results` checks numerical files, figure layout and provenance bindings. Task identifiers, data and plotting entry points correspond directly.

W1 uses the [data-reading entry point](../scripts/plot_w1_original_style.py) to connect the [error-curve function](../experiments/formal/w1_recursive_prediction/plot_mean_error.py) and [keyframe function](../experiments/formal/w1_recursive_prediction/plot_keyframes.py). W2 uses its [data-reading entry point](../experiments/additional/w2_original_style_figures.py) to connect the [cross-grid plotting function](../experiments/formal/w2_cross_resolution/plot_results.py). Inputs are completed evaluation reports in numeric form and saved prediction arrays; outputs are curves, field panels and provenance manifests for each evaluation set. Each entry point's `--help` lists input and new-output-directory arguments.
